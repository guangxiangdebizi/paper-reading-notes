## 一、§6.3 English Constituency Parsing（Table 4）

### 原文 1（任务选择与动机）

> *"To evaluate if the Transformer can generalize to other tasks we performed experiments on English constituency parsing. This task presents specific challenges: the output is subject to strong structural constraints and is significantly longer than the input. Furthermore, RNN sequence-to-sequence models have not been able to attain state-of-the-art results in small-data regimes [37]."*

**直译：** 为评估 Transformer 能否泛化到其他任务，做了**英语成分句法分析**实验。该任务有特定挑战：输出受**强结构约束**，且**显著长于输入**。此外，RNN 的 seq2seq 模型在小数据条件下未能达到 SOTA [37]。

**解读：** 这个实验的目的不是再刷一个 SOTA，而是回答"架构是否任务通用"。任务选择本身很有针对性：

1. **输出受强结构约束**：句法分析的输出是合法的句法树（括号序列），不是自由文本——考验模型能否学到硬约束；
2. **输出显著长于输入**：树比句子长——考验 decoder 处理变长输出的能力；
3. **小数据条件下 RNN seq2seq 不行**（引文 [37]，Vinyals & Kaiser et al. "Grammar as a Foreign Language"）——Transformer 若能在 40K 句的小语料上做好，就证明其优势不依赖大数据。

### 原文 2（实验设置）

> *"We trained a 4-layer transformer with d_model = 1024 on the Wall Street Journal (WSJ) portion of the Penn Treebank [25], about 40K training sentences. We also trained it in a semi-supervised setting, using the larger high-confidence and BerkleyParser corpora from with approximately 17M sentences [37]. We used a vocabulary of 16K tokens for the WSJ only setting and a vocabulary of 32K tokens for the semi-supervised setting."*

> *"We performed only a small number of experiments to select the dropout, both attention and residual (section 5.4), learning rates and beam size on the Section 22 development set, all other parameters remained unchanged from the English-to-German base translation model. During inference, we increased the maximum output length to input length + 300. We used a beam size of 21 and α = 0.3 for both WSJ only and the semi-supervised setting."*

**直译：** 在 Penn Treebank [25] 的 WSJ 部分训练了 **4 层** Transformer（**d_model=1024**），约 **40K 训练句**。也在半监督设置下训练，用更大的 high-confidence 与 BerkleyParser 语料（约 **1700 万句**）[37]。仅 WSJ 用 16K 词表，半监督用 32K。只在 Section 22 开发集上做**少量实验**调 dropout（注意力与残差两处）、学习率与 beam 大小，**其余参数与英德 base 翻译模型完全一致**。推理时把最大输出长度提高到输入长度 + 300，两个设置都用 **beam=21、α=0.3**。

**解读：** 这段设置透露了论文的核心论点——**"缺乏任务特化调参"（lack of task-specific tuning）**：

1. **只改了三处**：层数 6→4、d_model 512→1024、推理输出长度 +50→+300；
2. **调参范围极小**：只在开发集上"少量实验"调了 dropout、学习率、beam size 三个超参，其余不动；
3. **对比设置的公平性**：半监督设置用了 1700 万句，与 RNN 系论文的半监督设置对齐——确保不是数据优势。

### 原文 3（结果与论断）

> *"Our results in Table 4 show that despite the lack of task-specific tuning our model performs surprisingly well, yielding better results than all previously reported models with the exception of the Recurrent Neural Network Grammar [8]."*

> *"In contrast to RNN sequence-to-sequence models [37], the Transformer outperforms the BerkeleyParser [29] even when training only on the WSJ training set of 40K sentences."*

**直译：** Table 4 的结果表明，尽管缺乏任务特化调参，模型表现**出乎意料地好**——除了 Recurrent Neural Network Grammar [8] 之外，超过所有此前报告的模型。与 RNN seq2seq 模型 [37] 不同，即便只用 **40K 句**的 WSJ 训练集训练，Transformer 也**超过 BerkeleyParser** [29]。

**解读：** Table 4 的读法：

| Parser | Training | WSJ 23 F1 |
|---|---|---|
| Vinyals & Kaiser et al. (2014) [37] | WSJ only, discriminative | 88.3 |
| Petrov et al. (2006) [29] | WSJ only, discriminative | 90.4 |
| Zhu et al. (2013) [40] | WSJ only, discriminative | 90.4 |
| Dyer et al. (2016) [8] | WSJ only, discriminative | 91.7 |
| **Transformer (4 layers)** | WSJ only, discriminative | **91.3** |
| Zhu et al. (2013) [40] | semi-supervised | 91.3 |
| Huang & Harper (2009) [14] | semi-supervised | 91.3 |
| McClosky et al. (2006) [26] | semi-supervised | 92.1 |
| Vinyals & Kaiser et al. (2014) [37] | semi-supervised | 92.1 |
| **Transformer (4 layers)** | semi-supervised | **92.7** |
| Luong et al. (2015) [23] | multi-task | 93.0 |
| Dyer et al. (2016) [8] | generative | 93.3 |

三个结论：

1. **半监督 92.7**：超过所有半监督模型（此前最佳 McClosky / Vinyals 的 92.1），仅次于用 multi-task（93.0）与 generative（93.3）两种不同范式的系统——即 Transformer 用 discriminative 范式拿到了该范式下的最好成绩；
2. **WSJ only 91.3**：与 Dyer et al. 的 91.7 几乎持平、超过 Vinyals & Kaiser 的 RNN seq2seq（88.3）与 BerkeleyParser（90.4）——**小数据条件下不输专门设计的模型**；
3. **泛化论断**：这是摘要"generalizes well to other tasks"的兑现。这个结论在事后看意义远超原文：Transformer 从"翻译模型"升格为"通用序列转换架构"，为后续 BERT/GPT/ViT 等所有方向的扩展奠定了合法性。

## 二、§7 Conclusion

### 原文 4

> *"In this work, we presented the Transformer, the first sequence transduction model based entirely on attention, replacing the recurrent layers most commonly used in encoder-decoder architectures with multi-headed self-attention."*

> *"For translation tasks, the Transformer can be trained significantly faster than architectures based on recurrent or convolutional layers. On both WMT 2014 English-to-German and WMT 2014 English-to-French translation tasks, we achieve a new state of the art. In the former task our best model outperforms even all previously reported ensembles."*

> *"We are excited about the future of attention-based models and plan to apply them to other tasks. We plan to extend the Transformer to problems involving input and output modalities other than text and to investigate local, restricted attention mechanisms to efficiently handle large inputs and outputs such as images, audio and video. Making generation less sequential is another research goals of ours."*

> *"The code we used to train and evaluate our models is available at https://github.com/tensorflow/tensor2tensor."*

**直译：** 本文提出 Transformer——第一个完全基于注意力的序列转换模型，用多头自注意力替换了 encoder-decoder 架构中最常用的循环层。在翻译任务上，Transformer 的训练显著快于基于循环或卷积层的架构；在两个 WMT 2014 任务上都达到新 SOTA，英德方向上最佳模型甚至超过所有此前报告的集成。我们对注意力模型的未来感到兴奋，计划应用到其他任务：**扩展到文本以外的输入/输出模态**，**研究局部、受限的注意力机制以高效处理图像、音频、视频等大输入/输出**；**让生成更少串行**是另一个研究目标。代码开源于 github.com/tensorflow/tensor2tensor。

**解读：** 结论三段的读法：

1. **第一段重申定位**：first ... based entirely on attention——与 §2 的 "To the best of our knowledge" 呼应；
2. **第二段重申成绩**：两个 WMT 任务 SOTA、英德超所有集成、训练更快——与 §6.1 一致；
3. **第三段是全文最被低估的一段**，三个未来方向在事后全部兑现：
   - **"extend to modalities other than text"**——ViT/DETR/MAE 等视觉 Transformer 的直系预告；
   - **"investigate local, restricted attention ... images, audio and video"**——局部/受限注意力（第 2 站 Table 1 的 restricted self-attention 一行、第 3 站 §6.2 (B) 行的自我保留）在此升格为明确研究方向，后来的 Sparse/Longformer/Swin 窗口注意力均为其兑现；
   - **"Making generation less sequential"**——非自回归生成的预告（后来的非自回归翻译、扩散模型等）。
   
   这段结论因此可视为整个 Transformer 时代的"路线图"——作者列出的三个方向，恰好是后来五年视觉 Transformer、高效注意力、非自回归生成三条主线的起点。
4. **开源代码**：tensor2tensor——这是论文影响力快速扩散的关键，与 ViT/DETR 时代的 DETR 开源一脉相承。

## 三、附录 Attention Visualizations（Figure 3–5）

### Figure 3（`fig3_page13.png`）：长距离依赖

> *"An example of the attention mechanism following long-distance dependencies in the encoder self-attention in layer 5 of 6. Many of the attention heads attend to a distant dependency of the verb 'making', completing the phrase 'making...more difficult'. Attentions here shown only for the word 'making'. Different colors represent different heads. Best viewed in color."*

**直译：** encoder 第 5 层（共 6 层）self-attention 跟随长距离依赖的例子。许多注意力头关注动词 'making' 的一个远距离依赖，补全短语 'making...more difficult'。图中只展示 'making' 一词的注意力，不同颜色代表不同头。

**解读：** 输入句 "It is in this spirit that a majority of American governments have passed new laws since 2009 **making** the registration or voting process more difficult"。'making' 的远距离依赖是 'more difficult'——两者在句中相距较远，但多个头都把注意力从 'making' 指向 'more difficult'。这正是第 2 站 §4 "路径长度 O(1)"论断的可视化证据：RNN 要让 'making' 与 'more difficult' 建立关联，信号需沿链条走多步；self-attention 一步直达，且不同头各自学不同的依赖模式。

### Figure 4（`fig4_page14.png`）：指代消解（anaphora resolution）

> *"Two attention heads, also in layer 5 of 6, apparently involved in anaphora resolution. Top: Full attentions for head 5. Bottom: Isolated attentions from just the word 'its' for attention heads 5 and 6. Note that the attentions are very sharp for this word."*

**直译：** 两个注意力头（同样在第 5 层）显然参与了**指代消解**。上图：head 5 的完整注意力。下图：仅 'its' 一词在 head 5 与 head 6 中的注意力。注意 'its' 的注意力非常尖锐。

**解读：** 输入句 "The Law will never be perfect, but **its** application should be just"。'its' 是代词，指代 'The Law'。图中 head 5 与 head 6 都把 'its' 的注意力尖锐地指向 'The Law'——**不同头各自学会了指代消解这一语言学能力**。这是"self-attention 可解释性"论断（§4 末段）的第二个证据：不同头各自学不同的语言功能。

### Figure 5（`fig5_page15.png`）：句法结构

> *"Many of the attention heads exhibit behaviour that seems related to the structure of the sentence. We give two such examples above, from two different heads from the encoder self-attention at layer 5 of 6. The heads clearly learned to perform different tasks."*

**直译：** 许多注意力头表现出似乎与**句子结构**相关的行为。上图给出两个这样的例子，来自 encoder 第 5 层的两个不同头。这些头显然学会了执行不同的任务。

**解读：** Figure 5 展示两个头分别学到句法依存关系（如动词-宾语、名词-修饰语等结构）。与 Figure 3/4 合起来，三个图共同支撑 §4 末段的论断："individual attention heads clearly learn to perform different tasks, many appear to exhibit behavior related to the syntactic and semantic structure of the sentences"。

这三个图在论文中的作用：**为 self-attention 的可解释性提供实证**——注意力权重不是黑箱，而是可以直接读出语言学意义的结构。这也是后来 ViT 等视觉 Transformer 研究 attention 可视化（如 attention rollout、attention map）的源头。
